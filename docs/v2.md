## Recommendations

In the recommendations page, ask people if they would like help to achieve their goals. If so, they can enter their name and contact information and click "Request help", which will send their responses to a team of professional researchers and deliberation designers.

Don't worry about displaying recommendations yet, but here are some placeholders:
Face-to-face: may cost more, but can also increase participation if recruitment is done correctly (e.g., face-to-face).
Invited only: can control the demographics (equality), but might reduce inclusion by limiting participation
Self-selection: may be more inclusive, but may decrease equality -- it is possible to achieve both if using targeted recruiting tactics here
